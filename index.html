About Our School

Who we are and what we stand for

🏫 Our Story

Iriebe Town Secondary School (UBE) is a public junior secondary school in Iriebe Town, Port Harcourt, Rivers State, established under the Universal Basic Education programme. We serve JSS 1 to JSS 3 and prepare our students for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) and senior secondary education.

🎯 Our Mission

To give every child a solid foundation in learning, character and skills, regardless of background.

👁️ Our Vision

A school where every child in our community is educated, confident and ready for the future.

💚 Our Values

Discipline, hard work, honesty, respect and service to our community.

👩🏾‍🏫 Our Teachers

Our dedicated teachers are qualified, caring and committed to every child's progress. Class sizes are kept manageable so no child is left behind.

Academics

Classes and subjects we offer

JSS 1

Foundation year — settling into secondary school life with strong basics in English, Mathematics and Science.

JSS 2

Building year — deeper subject knowledge and the start of vocational and business studies.

JSS 3

Final year — intensive preparation for the BECE and guidance into senior secondary school.

📚 Subjects

  • English Language  ·  Mathematics  ·  Basic Science  ·  Basic Technology
  • Social Studies  ·  Civic Education  ·  Agricultural Science
  • Business Studies  ·  Computer Studies (ICT)
  • Cultural & Creative Arts  ·  Christian Religious Studies
  • Physical & Health Education  ·  Home Economics  ·  French

Admissions

How to enrol your child into JSS 1

1
Get the form

Collect the admission form from the school office (weekdays, 8am–2pm).

2
Bring the documents

Primary school certificate, birth certificate, and two passport photographs.

3
Placement test

Your child sits a short placement test in English and Mathematics.

4
Enrolment

Successful candidates complete registration and receive their exam number — which also works on this website's result checker.
