Iriebe Town
Secondary School

Iriebe Town Secondary School (UBE) is a government secondary school located in Iriebe Town, Port Harcourt, Rivers State, serving the community under the Universal Basic Education programme. The school is committed to providing quality, free and compulsory basic education to every child.

With dedicated teachers and a safe learning environment, the school prepares students in the junior secondary classes for success in senior secondary education and beyond, instilling the values of hard work, discipline and service to the community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. As a UBE school, tuition is completely free under the Universal Basic Education programme. Parents only provide basic personal items like uniforms, sandals and writing materials.

We are a junior secondary school, covering JSS 1 to JSS 3, after which students sit the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

Your ward should have completed Primary 6. Bring the primary school testimonial or last result, a birth certificate or age declaration, and two passport photographs to the school office to register.

School runs Monday to Friday. Assembly begins at 7:30am and closing is at 2:30pm. Students are expected on the assembly ground by 7:20am.
