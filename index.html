Welcome from the Head Teacher

Iriebe Town Secondary School (UBE) is a junior secondary school serving the families of Iriebe Town and the surrounding communities of Port Harcourt. We believe every child can succeed when they are taught well, disciplined kindly and encouraged daily.

Our doors are open to parents — visit us, ask questions, and follow your child's progress. With our online result checker, you can view your child's terminal report from anywhere using a simple PIN.
