About Our School

Who we are, what we stand for, and the people who lead Iriebe Town Secondary School (UBE).

Our Story

Iriebe Town Secondary School (UBE) is a junior secondary school serving Iriebe Town and its neighbouring communities in Obio/Akpor, Port Harcourt, Rivers State. From our first day, our purpose has been simple: to give every child a strong academic foundation and the character to stand tall anywhere in the world.

Under the Universal Basic Education (UBE) programme, we provide quality, accessible junior secondary education — JSS 1 to JSS 3 — with dedicated teachers, a safe environment and a curriculum that prepares our students for senior secondary school and beyond.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To provide quality basic education that develops every student academically, morally and socially, regardless of background — in a safe, disciplined and caring environment.

👁️ Our Vision

To be the leading junior secondary school in our community, raising confident, well-educated young people who excel in further education and in life.

🏅 Our Motto

Knowledge, Discipline and Excellence — the three pillars on which every day at our school is built.

Our Core Values

  • Discipline — punctuality, neatness and respect in and out of the classroom.
  • Hard work — we teach students that results come from effort.
  • Honesty — integrity in examinations, conduct and character.
  • Respect — for teachers, parents, fellow students and the community.
  • Excellence — always aiming higher than yesterday.
