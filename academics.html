Academics

A solid junior secondary foundation — classes, subjects, terms and how we measure progress.

Classes We Offer

📗 JSS 1

The entry class. Students settle into secondary school life, build strong basics in English, Mathematics and Science, and learn our culture of discipline and hard work.

📘 JSS 2

The consolidation class. Knowledge deepens across all subjects, practical skills grow, and students begin serious preparation for the Basic Education Certificate Examination.

📕 JSS 3

The graduation class. Focused revision, mock examinations and the Junior WAEC (BECE), preparing students for a confident move into senior secondary school.

Subjects We Teach

We follow the Nigerian basic education curriculum for junior secondary schools:

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Basic Science and Technology
  • Social Studies
  • Civic Education
  • Business Studies
  • Agricultural Science
  • Christian Religious Studies
  • Cultural and Creative Arts
  • Physical and Health Education
  • Computer Studies (ICT)
  • Nigerian Languages

The School Year

🗓️ First Term

September to December. New students are welcomed, the year's work begins, and the term closes with examinations and the Christmas break.

🗓️ Second Term

January to March/April. Teaching continues in earnest, with mid-term tests, inter-house sports and end-of-term examinations.

🗓️ Third Term

April/May to July. Revision, promotion examinations and — for JSS 3 — the BECE. The session ends with our prize-giving and graduation.

Assessment & Results

Students are assessed continuously through classwork, assignments, mid-term tests and end-of-term examinations. At the end of every term, each student receives a full result sheet.

Results are published on this website. Every student receives a confidential PIN, and parents can view and print their child's result from anywhere — phone or computer — using the exam number and PIN.
