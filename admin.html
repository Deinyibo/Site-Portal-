Site Colors

Pick new colors — every page changes at once after you save. The preview below shows your combination.

Preview: headings, buttons and banners use these colors. Check Result Latest

School Logo

Shown in the header of every page. Upload a square PNG/JPG for best results (about 300×300). This is the same slot as the logo entry on the Photos tab.

Website Photos

For each photo slot, either upload a picture (kept inside your settings — keep each under ~800 KB) or type its path after uploading the file to the images/ folder (e.g. via GitHub). Clear both and save to remove a photo — the page shows its green placeholder again.

Page Text

Every changeable text on the site, page by page. Leave a field empty to keep the text already built into the page. Use the filter to find one quickly.

Add a Box

Boxes appear at the bottom of the page you choose — above the green call-to-action band. Use them for announcements, reminders, exam timetables… anything.

Post News

News cards appear on the News page — and on the homepage once the homepage patch is applied. Until you post your first card, the pages show their built-in starter cards.

📢 Scrolling Announcement (ticker)

Runs along the top of every page. Separate announcements with a dash (—). Leave empty to show the built-in default.

📊 Homepage Numbers (stats band)

The big gold numbers on the homepage. Leave any empty to keep the current one. (These appear once the homepage patch is applied.)

Contact Details

Shown in the footer of every page and on the Contact page. Leave empty to keep the built-in text.

Social Media Handles

Shown on the Contact page and in the footer "Follow Us" column. Leave empty to keep the current handle.

⚠️ Changing a handle here updates the text visitors see. The actual links (facebook.com/…, wa.me/…) live in the page code — tell me the real handles and I'll update the links themselves, one line each.
