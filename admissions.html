Admissions

Joining Iriebe Town Secondary School (UBE) is simple — here is everything you need to know.

Who Can Apply

Admission is open into JSS 1, JSS 2 and JSS 3. Entry into JSS 1 is for pupils who have completed Primary 6; transfer students are welcome into JSS 2 and JSS 3 at the start of a term, subject to space.

As a UBE junior secondary school, we serve our community first — every child from Iriebe Town and the surrounding areas has a place here. Parents from further afield are equally welcome to apply.

Ask About Admission

How to Apply — Step by Step

1

Visit the school. Come to the school office during school hours (Monday–Friday, 7:30am–2:00pm) and collect an admission form. You can also call or message us first using the Contact page.

2

Fill and return the form. Return the completed form with the documents listed below — birth certificate, last result/report card, and passport photographs.

3

Placement test. Your child sits a short placement test in English and Mathematics so we can place them in the right class and support them well.

4

Admission & registration. Successful candidates receive an admission letter, pay the approved charges, and complete registration. Your child receives their exam number — keep it safe; it is used for checking results online.

What to Bring

  • Birth certificate (photocopy)
  • Last school result / report card (for transfer students)
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Completed admission form
  • Parent or guardian's phone number

Fees & Charges

As a UBE school, tuition is free. Only small approved charges apply (for items such as the admission form, PTA levy and examination registration). Please confirm the current amounts at the school office — the Admin can also update this note from the dashboard at any time.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does admission open?

Admission for a new session opens before First Term begins (around August–September). Transfers into JSS 2 and JSS 3 are considered at the start of any term, subject to space.

Can my child transfer from another school?

Yes. Bring the last result or report card and a transfer letter from the previous school, and the child will take our placement test.

How do I check my child's result after admission?

Every student receives an exam number and a confidential PIN. Any term, you can open the Check Result page on this website, enter the exam number and PIN, and view or print the result.
